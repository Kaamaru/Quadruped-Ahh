CRITICAL POWER NOTICE: External Servo Power Required

Standard servos (like SG90 or MG996R) can draw 500mA to 2A under load. Do NOT power multiple servos directly from the 5V output of an Arduino or ESP32 board! This will trigger brownout resets or burn out your board's regulator. Connect a dedicated external 5V/6V DC power supply (3A–5A minimum) to the PCA9685 V+ screw terminal block. Keep the Ground (GND) lines connected together (Common Ground).
